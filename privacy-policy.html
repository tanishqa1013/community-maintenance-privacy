Privacy Policy — Community Maintenance

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy covers the Community Maintenance app ("the app"). The app is used independently by many different residential communities (gated communities, housing societies, residents' welfare associations). Each community's management committee operates its own community inside the app; one community's data is never visible to another.

Who this policy is for

If you are a resident or administrator of a community that uses this app, this policy describes how your community's data is collected, used and protected. If you manage a community using this app, you (and your fellow administrators) control what residents' data your community holds — the app's developer does not see or use it beyond providing the app itself.

What information the app collects

From every account (resident or administrator): - Mobile number (used to sign in — the app does not use email or social login) - Full name - Plot/house number and resident type (owner or tenant) - A password, stored securely by Firebase Authentication (Google infrastructure) — never visible to anyone, including the app's developer or your community's administrators

From residents, when making or viewing payments: - Maintenance payment records: amount, month, payment mode (UPI, bank transfer or cash), and the UPI/bank transaction reference you enter - A photo of a payment confirmation, only if you submit one as proof — chosen from your phone's photo gallery; the app does not use your camera directly - Your full payment and receipt history within your own community

From administrators, additionally: - Your community's own settings: name, address, registration number, logo, bank/UPI details, maintenance amount and due dates — set by your community, not by the app's developer - A record of administrative actions you take (verifying a payment, resetting a resident's password, and so on), kept as an audit log visible to your community's other administrators

Automatically, for every signed-in device: - A device identifier used only to deliver push notifications for your community's announcements (you can turn notifications off in your phone's settings) - If the app crashes, a crash report containing the technical error and your device model and OS version — never your name, mobile number, or anything you entered in the app - Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): which screens are opened, how often the app is used, whether a payment was submitted or a residents list exported, and the type of device and app version. Each installation gets a random app-instance identifier that is not linked to your name, mobile number, plot or community. Google derives an approximate country/city from the connection. No names, mobile numbers, plots, amounts or receipt details are ever included.

What the app does NOT collect: your phone's contacts, your location, your camera roll (beyond a photo you explicitly choose to upload), or any advertising identifier (advertising-ID collection and ad personalisation are switched off). The app shows no ads.

Who can see your data

  • Your name, plot number and mobile number are visible to your own community's administrators, and to other residents of your own community in the Residents Directory (for contacting neighbours) — never to anyone outside your community.
  • Your payment and receipt records are visible to you and to your community's administrators only.
  • Nothing is ever visible across different communities using the app. This is enforced by the app's server, not only by the screens you see.

Where your data is stored

All data is stored using Google Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure), transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The app's developer does not operate separate servers.

How long data is kept

  • Payment and receipt records are kept indefinitely, the same way a physical receipt book would be, since they are your community's financial record. A receipt is never altered once issued.
  • If an administrator removes a resident's account, that resident's login is deleted, but their past receipts are kept as part of the community's financial history.
  • Uploaded images (payment proofs, your community's logo, signatures) are kept indefinitely once uploaded, so that a past receipt always displays exactly as it did when issued; the app does not provide a way to delete an uploaded image.

Your choices

  • You can ask your community's administrator to correct your name, plot number or resident type.
  • You can ask your community's administrator to remove your account. Your past payment records will be kept as part of the community's financial history, as described above.
  • You can turn off push notifications at any time in your phone's system settings.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for adult residents and administrators of residential communities and is not directed at children.
